What Claruno is — and isn't

Claruno reads a photo of handwritten math work using AI and gives feedback: whether it looks correct, where a mistake is, an explanation, and hints. It's a study aid, not a certified tutor or a replacement for a teacher.

The AI can be wrong. We work to make it accurate — including having it independently re-solve every problem before judging your work — but it can still misread handwriting, miss a mistake, or flag something that's actually correct. Don't treat a Claruno check as the final word on graded or high-stakes work; check with a teacher when it matters.
