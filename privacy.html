What this covers

This policy explains what information Claruno ("we", "us") collects when you use the app, why, and how it's handled. "You" means anyone using Claruno, including students, parents, and teachers.

Information we collect

Claruno works without an account for a single check. Creating an account (email/password or "Continue with Google") unlocks history, lessons, and the dashboard, and involves more data:

  • Account info: email address, and — if you sign in with Google — the name and email Google shares with us.
  • Photos of handwritten work: images you upload or capture with your camera, sent for AI analysis. If you're using the live lesson camera, only the frame you actually check or the frame behind a hint is captured and sent — the video is never streamed or recorded continuously.
  • Photos of printed problems: if you choose to attach a photo of a textbook/worksheet problem alongside your work.
  • Check history: the AI's verdict, explanation, and any follow-up questions you ask about a check, saved so it's available across your devices.
  • Profile details: anything you choose to enter in Profile — name, school, class, country, language, a weekly lesson schedule, and upcoming test dates.
  • Usage data: lesson start/end times and duration, so the Dashboard can show streaks and weekly progress.

We do not knowingly collect more than what's listed above, and we don't run advertising trackers or sell data to third parties.

How your photos are handled

A photo you check is sent to our server, which forwards it to Anthropic's Claude API to be read and evaluated — Anthropic processes it as a data processor on our behalf and does not use it to identify you. The image and the AI's response are then saved to your account's history (as a compressed thumbnail, with the mark it drew baked in) so you can look back at it later. You can't currently delete an individual history entry from the app itself; contact us (below) to request deletion.

Camera captures try to exclude anything besides the paper itself, but nothing automatically detects or removes a hand, background, or other people who happen to be in frame — please keep the camera pointed only at your own written work.

Where data is stored

  • Supabase hosts the account database (auth, history, profile). Access is restricted so each account can only read or write its own rows.
  • Anthropic processes photos and text to generate checks, hints, and lesson content.
  • Google, only if you choose "Continue with Google", handles that sign-in.
  • Vercel or Netlify hosts the app and the serverless functions that talk to the above.

No payment or billing information is collected — Claruno does not currently charge for anything.
